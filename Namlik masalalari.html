1. 20 °C da havoning bug' zichligi 10,4 g/m³. φ ni toping.
Berilgan

Formula
φ = ρ / ρ₀ · 100 %
Hisoblash

Javob

2. 25 °C da φ = 40 %. Bug' zichligi ρ nechaga teng?
Berilgan
t = 25 °C, φ = 40 %, ρ₀ = 23,0 g/m³
Formula
ρ = φ · ρ₀
Hisoblash
ρ = 0,40 · 23,0 = 9,2 g/m³
Javob
ρ = 9,2 g/m³
3. Xona 6 m × 5 m × 3 m, t = 20 °C, φ = 50 %. Havodagi bug' massasi qancha?
Berilgan
V = 6·5·3 = 90 m³, ρ₀ = 17,3 g/m³, φ = 50 %
Formula
ρ = φ · ρ₀, m = ρ · V
Hisoblash
ρ = 0,5 · 17,3 = 8,65 g/m³; m = 8,65 · 90 ≈ 779 g
Javob
m ≈ 0,78 kg
4. 25 °C da bug' zichligi 12,8 g/m³. φ va shudring nuqtasini toping.
Berilgan
t = 25 °C, ρ = 12,8 g/m³, ρ₀(25 °C) = 23,0 g/m³
Formula
φ = ρ / ρ₀ · 100 %; shudring nuqtasida ρ₀ = ρ
Hisoblash
φ = 12,8 / 23,0 · 100 % ≈ 56 %. Jadvalda ρ₀ = 12,8 g/m³ 15 °C ga to'g'ri keladi.
Javob
φ ≈ 56 %, shudring nuqtasi 15 °C
5. Psixrometr: quruq 22 °C, nam 16 °C. φ qancha?
Berilgan
t₁ = 22 °C, t₂ = 16 °C
Formula
Δt = t₁ − t₂, so'ng psixrometrik jadval
Hisoblash
Δt = 6 °C. Jadvalda t₁ = 22 qator va Δt = 6 ustun kesishmasi taxminan 53 %.
Javob
φ ≈ 53 %
6. 30 °C, φ = 50 %. Stakan sirti qaysi haroratdan pastga tushganda unda shudring paydo bo'ladi?
Berilgan
t = 30 °C, φ = 50 %, ρ₀ = 30,3 g/m³
Formula
ρ = φ · ρ₀; shudring nuqtasida ρ₀(t_sh) = ρ
Hisoblash
ρ = 0,5 · 30,3 ≈ 15,2 g/m³. Jadvalda 15,2 g/m³ 15 °C (12,8) va 20 °C (17,3) orasida, taxminan 18 °C.
